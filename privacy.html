Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tansi Cyril collects, uses, stores and protects information when you visit this website or use features connected to the website.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Tansi Cyril, a video editor and digital content creator.

The website is used to showcase professional video editing work, creative projects, testimonials, social media content and related professional information.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect or receive limited information such as:

  • Your name and contact information when you voluntarily contact us.
  • Email addresses and other information included in messages you send us.
  • Information required to operate authorized social media integrations.
  • Technical information that may be automatically provided by your browser or hosting infrastructure.
  • Public information made available through an authorized social media account or API.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to professional inquiries and messages.
  • Provide and maintain the website.
  • Display portfolio projects and related information.
  • Operate authorized social media integrations.
  • Retrieve and display authorized TikTok profile or video information.
  • Retrieve and display permitted social media content or performance information.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the website.
  • Improve the website and its features.

4. TikTok integration

This website may use TikTok for Developers products, including Login Kit and the TikTok Display API.

If a user chooses to connect a TikTok account, TikTok may ask the user to authorize access to specific information requested by the application.

For the current integration, the requested permissions may include:

  • user.info.basic — used to access basic authorized TikTok profile information such as the display name, avatar and account identifier.
  • video.list — used to access the authorized user's public TikTok videos.

TikTok's Display API provides APIs for retrieving a user's basic profile information and public videos. [oai_citation:1‡TikTok for Developers](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/display-api-overview?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

We only request permissions necessary for the features described in this Privacy Policy and the TikTok integration.

We do not sell TikTok account information to third parties.

5. TikTok authorization

TikTok authorization is handled through TikTok's own authorization interface.

When authorization is completed, TikTok provides the application with an authorization code. The server-side application exchanges that code for an access token and, where applicable, a refresh token.

TikTok's Login Kit documentation requires applications to keep the client secret and refresh token securely stored on the server. [oai_citation:2‡TikTok for Developers](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/login-kit-web?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

We use server-side controls to protect these credentials and do not intentionally expose private API credentials through the public website.

Social media statistics are controlled by the respective platforms and may change after they are displayed on this website.
